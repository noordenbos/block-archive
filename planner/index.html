PHOTO TRANSFER

From your phone to this laptop

This app reads photos from a folder on this laptop. Nothing is sent to a cloud service. Use your institution-approved capture and transfer workflow for identifiable material.

1. Photograph each block

  1. Print and attach the human ID beside its matching QR code.
  2. Take one identifier-side photo showing the rim and QR/ID.
  3. Turn the block over and take one tissue-side photo showing the scoring mat, ruler and TOP / LABEL END marker.
  4. Keep the whole mat in frame, camera overhead, lens clean, and lighting even. Do not crop or mirror the photos.

2. Move the photos to this laptop

  • Managed phone: use the capture app and transfer route approved by your institution. Keep each block’s two photos together.
  • USB: connect the phone, open Image Capture (Mac) or the phone’s file-transfer tool, and import the images to a new folder.
  • Other phones: use the phone’s USB file transfer or an institution-approved transfer method, then copy both images into one folder.

3. Ingest the folder

  1. Keep both views in the same folder; filenames and order do not matter.
  2. Click Ingest photo folder and choose that folder.
  3. The app reads QR values, pairs tissue and identifier views, rotates them to TOP up, and estimates planar scale automatically.
  4. Review the ingest report. Unmatched, ambiguous or conflicting photos stay listed for correction; clear pairs need no confirmation.
File formats: JPEG, PNG and WebP are accepted. Convert HEIC locally before ingest. The browser stores photos and project backups in this laptop’s local browser storage. Spatial Prep has no built-in authentication or encryption at rest; follow your institution’s approved PHI transfer and retention policy for real samples.
