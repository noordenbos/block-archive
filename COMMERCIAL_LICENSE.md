To discuss a license, contact the [repository maintainer](https://github.com/noordenbos) through a contact channel on that profile. Include your organization, intended use, deployment scale, and whether you plan to distribute or incorporate the software. Do not include patient information, specimen IDs or project backups.

Uses already permitted by PolyForm do not require a separate paid license. In particular, the license includes permissions for educational, public research and other listed organizations regardless of funding. The full license controls those permissions.
